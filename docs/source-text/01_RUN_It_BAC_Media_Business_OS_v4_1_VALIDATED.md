# 1. Identity and Strategic Position
RUN It BAC Media is a revenue infrastructure company. Its proprietary method is Revenue Spine. The company diagnoses where a buyer journey or operating workflow loses economic value, prescribes the minimum intervention that closes the leak, installs the system, measures what changed, and compounds the result through optimization.
| COMMERCIAL POSITIONING / We diagnose where revenue gets stuck, install the missing infrastructure, and track the improvement. |
## What RUN It BAC is not
Not a generic web-design agency.
Not an AI-tools reseller.
Not a broad marketing-services menu.
Not a custom-work shop where every engagement starts from zero.
Not a community brand disguised as a sales funnel.
## The role of Revenue Spine
Revenue Spine is the common method across local-service conversion systems, AI-enabled workflow improvement, institutional training, optimization, and future software. The method remains stable while the intervention changes.

# 7. Delivery and Learning Loop
Capture baseline evidence and operating metrics before implementation.
Install only the interventions included in the prescription.
Run QA against scope, evidence, and launch requirements.
Record launch state and first measurable operating changes.
At 30 days, compare baseline, current state, and target state.
Classify what improved, what did not, and what cannot yet be determined.
Convert verified outcomes into case-study data, benchmark data, and better intervention rules.
Feed learnings back into the diagnostic framework without overwriting historical versions.
# 8. Founder Capacity Locks
Founder attention, not calendar capacity, is the binding constraint.
Prime founder time is reserved for selling, diagnosis, closing, product architecture, teaching/facilitation, and key relationships.
No new service is added before delivery for the current stack is standardized or delegated.
Low-ticket custom creative work is killed unless it directly supports a Revenue Spine engagement.
One strong month is not proof of durable enterprise economics.
Collected revenue, booked revenue, recurring revenue, gross margin, and founder delivery hours are tracked separately.
| CAPACITY TRIGGER / Delegate before adding offers. If founder-only delivery exceeds the defined weekly cap for two consecutive weeks, new service design freezes until capacity is restored. |

# Market Reality and Confidence Framework
The 2026 market strengthens the case for Revenue Spine, but it does not justify assuming demand. Small businesses are under cost pressure and increasingly prioritize efficiency, stability, and deeper customer relationships. AI use is widespread, while scaled ROI remains uneven. The operating consequence is clear: RUN It BAC should sell measurable constraint removal, not abstract transformation or AI novelty.
| 2026 Signal | Implication for RUN It BAC |
| Small-business AI use is widespread, but many firms remain early in practical adoption. | Do not sell AI access. Sell one measurable workflow or revenue constraint solved. |
| Cost, unclear ROI, fragmented data, and workforce readiness are major blockers. | Start with bounded interventions, explicit baselines, and human-in-the-loop adoption. |
| Small businesses face high prices and are cautious about expansion and capital spending. | Reduce upfront scope; use fast payback logic and staged expansion after proof. |
| Businesses increasingly want stability and stronger customer relationships. | Emphasize conversion, retention, response, follow-up, and operational reliability. |
| Enterprise AI use is broad, but most organizations are not yet scaling AI deeply into workflows. | Revenue Spine's workflow diagnosis + implementation layer is more defensible than generic AI consulting. |
| CONFIDENCE RULE / No artifact can make a startup 95% certain to produce revenue before the market validates willingness to pay, close rate, delivery margin, and retention. The objective is 95% confidence that the operating system will discover those answers quickly, protect cash, and scale only after evidence. |
## Startup Wedge: First 90 Days
Use one commercial wedge before expanding the full platform:
| WEDGE / Revenue Spine Conversion Infrastructure for high-value local and professional service businesses. |
Buyer: owner/CEO/COO with decision authority.
Business condition: already has meaningful traffic, referrals, advertising, or inquiry volume. Do not sell conversion infrastructure to a business with no demand source.
Economic condition: a small number of additional qualified bookings can plausibly repay the engagement.
Problem: weak message clarity, booking friction, slow response, fragmented follow-up, or missing measurement.
Promise: diagnose the highest-cost leak, install the minimum conversion infrastructure required, establish a baseline, and review measured change.
Boundary: no general branding retainers, social-media management, random AI agents, or large custom builds during the validation window.
## Sequential Expansion Rule
Prove the Local/Professional Service wedge.
Create at least three verified outcome records and a repeatable gross-margin profile.
Only then expand Revenue Spine to broader operational/AI workflows.
Only after repeatable services should external software licensing become a build priority.
## Validation Gates
| Gate | Threshold / Evidence | Action if Failed |
| Demand | At least 5 qualified buyer conversations in 30 days and at least 2 buyers advance to a commercial next step. | Change ICP/message/channel before adding features. |
| Willingness to Pay | At least 2 paid engagements or deposits from qualified buyers. | Do not call the offer validated. |
| Delivery | Scope delivered within promised window without founder overload. | Reduce scope or increase price / delegation. |
| Economics | Positive contribution margin and a clear path to the target gross-margin floor. | Redesign or kill the intervention. |
| Outcome | At least 3 clients produce verified operational or commercial improvement, or clear learning about why not. | Do not scale claims or paid acquisition. |
| Recurring | Optimization produces a defined ongoing job and clients retain because of measurable value. | Do not force a retainer that lacks a continuing job. |
