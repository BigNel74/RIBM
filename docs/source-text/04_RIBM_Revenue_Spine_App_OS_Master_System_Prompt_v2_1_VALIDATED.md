# 17. Version Control and Auditability
Every diagnostic stores framework, prompt, scoring, report-template, and intervention-library versions.
Historical reports remain reproducible.
No silent overwrite of finalized diagnostics.
Meaningful edits to finalized records create audit-log entries with user, timestamp, before/after, and reason.
AI model provider changes are implementation details, but material behavior changes require a prompt or framework version update.

# 28. Startup Validation and Confidence Engine
# Market Reality and Confidence Framework
The 2026 market strengthens the case for Revenue Spine, but it does not justify assuming demand. Small businesses are under cost pressure and increasingly prioritize efficiency, stability, and deeper customer relationships. AI use is widespread, while scaled ROI remains uneven. The operating consequence is clear: RUN It BAC should sell measurable constraint removal, not abstract transformation or AI novelty.
| 2026 Signal | Implication for RUN It BAC |
| Small-business AI use is widespread, but many firms remain early in practical adoption. | Do not sell AI access. Sell one measurable workflow or revenue constraint solved. |
| Cost, unclear ROI, fragmented data, and workforce readiness are major blockers. | Start with bounded interventions, explicit baselines, and human-in-the-loop adoption. |
| Small businesses face high prices and are cautious about expansion and capital spending. | Reduce upfront scope; use fast payback logic and staged expansion after proof. |
| Businesses increasingly want stability and stronger customer relationships. | Emphasize conversion, retention, response, follow-up, and operational reliability. |
| Enterprise AI use is broad, but most organizations are not yet scaling AI deeply into workflows. | Revenue Spine's workflow diagnosis + implementation layer is more defensible than generic AI consulting. |
| CONFIDENCE RULE / No artifact can make a startup 95% certain to produce revenue before the market validates willingness to pay, close rate, delivery margin, and retention. The objective is 95% confidence that the operating system will discover those answers quickly, protect cash, and scale only after evidence. |

## Startup Wedge Configuration
Default primary edition for the first 90 days: Local/Professional Service Conversion Infrastructure.
Do not expose all future editions as equal priorities in the operator UI.
Allow Admin to mark offers as PRIMARY, TEST, POST-SALE, DEFERRED, or RETIRED.

## Lead Qualification Rules
Reject or defer prospects with no meaningful demand source when the proposed intervention is conversion infrastructure.
Capture approximate average transaction/client value as CLIENT-PROVIDED, never inferred.
Capture monthly inquiry/lead volume only when known.
Require authority/decision process before generating a commercial proposal.
Flag low-value, high-customization work before proposal generation.
## Unit Economics Guardrails
Store estimated delivery hours, contractor cost, software cost, payment processing cost, and gross contribution by engagement.
Calculate realized gross margin after closeout.
Compare founder-only hours against the target capacity budget.
Do not mark an offer 'scalable' until multiple completed engagements show repeatable positive economics.
Pricing recommendations must be based on stored economics and buyer evidence, not generic industry markups.

## Confidence Scoring
Do not calculate a fake single probability that the business will succeed. Instead score confidence by dimension:
| Dimension | LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH |
| Demand | <3 qualified conversations | 3-9 qualified conversations with repeated pain | 10+ qualified conversations with repeated pain and commercial advancement |
| Willingness to Pay | No paid proof | 1 paid test | 3+ paid engagements at target scope/price |
| Delivery | Unbounded / founder-heavy | Delivered once with documented friction | 3+ repeatable deliveries within target capacity |
| Outcome | No verified outcome | 1-2 verified outcomes | 3+ comparable verified outcomes |
| Recurring | No continuing job | One retained client | Multiple retained clients with explicit ongoing job |
## Blue Ocean Evidence Rules
Store competitor/category factors separately from RUN It BAC factors.

Do not mark an offer 'Blue Ocean Validated' until buyers demonstrate reduced price-only comparison or select RUN It BAC specifically for the created factors.
Every Blue Ocean hypothesis must have a test date, target sample, success threshold, and kill condition.
